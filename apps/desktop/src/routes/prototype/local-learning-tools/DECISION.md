# P0.5 interaction decision

Selected: **Variant C, Study workspace**.

## Decisions to carry forward

- Keep writing and study work in explicit modes. The normal writing surface stays
  focused; learning tools open in a dedicated workspace.
- Keep the exact source passage visible and fixed during a learning session so
  coaching, optional local AI, quiz answers, and explanations remain grounded.
- Keep spelling available without entering Study mode. Replacements remain an
  explicit student action.
- Deterministic coaching identifies a passage and asks a focused question. The
  student supplies the reflection and edits the paper.
- Local AI remains optional, on-device, and limited to text the student selects.
  Spelling and deterministic coaching continue to work without a model.
- Grounded quizzes use fixed-choice answers and source-backed explanations. Quiz
  state is temporary unless a later approved design explicitly adds persistence.

## Prototype boundary

This decision records interaction intent, not production architecture or a
release promise. The prototype remains mock-only and must not be merged.
