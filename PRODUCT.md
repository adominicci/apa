# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University students are the primary users. Faculty members and researchers are
secondary users. They use Tesina to write academic papers in English or Spanish
without manually managing APA 7 formatting.

## Product Purpose

Tesina is a local-first academic word processor that produces submission-ready
APA 7 papers. Success means a student can focus on the paper's content while the
app reliably handles page structure, typography, citations, references, and
export without requiring an account or cloud storage.

## Brand Personality

Academically trustworthy, practical, and restrained. The interface should feel
like a dependable writing tool, with direct bilingual language and no visual
choices that compete with the document.

## Anti-references

- Never copy Essayist's name, code, text, or assets; Tesina is a clean-room
  functional alternative.
- Avoid flashy SaaS or AI-product styling that would weaken academic trust.
- Avoid copying examples or wording from the APA manual or "normas" websites.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the paper, not the application chrome, at the center of attention.
2. Make correct APA output the default instead of relying on users to remember
   formatting rules.
3. Preserve user ownership through local-first files and transparent exports.
4. Treat English and Spanish document conventions as equally complete.
5. Prefer familiar desktop writing-tool patterns over decorative novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

English and Spanish interfaces and document languages are first-class. Preserve
standard keyboard, focus, and assistive-technology behavior where the platform
and controls provide it, but WCAG 2.2 AA certification is not a first-release
requirement.
